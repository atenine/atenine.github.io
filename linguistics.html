linguistics

My main linguistic interests are scattered wherever I can find relations to speech. I am especially interested in how and why computer-workable representations of natural language data are structured the way they are.

projects:

Spatula:

see on GitHub

Uses torch to probe transformer-based audio encoder embeddings, over the TIMIT dataset. The hope is to develop our human intuition for how audio encoder models represent human understanding of acoustic features in speech data.

Deep Learning Disfluency Detection

see on GitHub

A deep learning-based disfluency detector for a seminar at SJSU. We extend work by Liu et al.(2023) and Mohapatra et al.(2022) for experimenting with multi-encoder disfluency detection using wav2vec, voc2vec, and Whisper. Trained on SEP-28k.

Silbo Gomero Spectrogrammetry

see on GitHub

A tool using Python and librosa to generate spectrograms for recordings of Silbo Gomero, a whistled language from the island of La Gomera.
